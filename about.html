About ShipDecKK

ShipDecKK was founded in 2020 with a passion for revolutionizing the maritime industry. Starting as a small startup offering yacht brokerage services, we quickly expanded into a full-service ship dealership, connecting customers with luxury liners, commercial cargo ships, and custom-built vessels around the world.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make ship ownership accessible, transparent, and enjoyable for enthusiasts and professionals alike. We strive to offer premium fleets, outstanding customer service, and innovative technology that bridges tradition and modern maritime solutions.

Our Values

  • ⚓ Excellence in Service — We are committed to delivering unmatched quality and customer satisfaction.
  • 🌍 Global Reach — We believe in connecting clients with ships across every ocean and port.
  • 🔧 Innovation — We embrace technology to improve buying, selling, and servicing experiences.
  • 🌱 Sustainability — We support eco-conscious shipping through green technologies and partnerships.

Our Achievements

  • 🏆 Delivered over 1,000 vessels worldwide within just 5 years.
  • 🌐 Established trusted partnerships in 15+ countries.
  • 🚢 Launched the first AI-powered yacht selection platform in 2023.
  • 💼 Featured in "Maritime Business Monthly" as a Top 10 Ship Retailer.
  • 👥 Achieved a 98% customer satisfaction rate based on post-sale surveys.
